Two documentation errors were corrected, not one. The obvious one: the approval gate did not
exist. The subtler one: every description of it had the ordering wrong — "build/test/ac-11 gates,
**then** waits for approval". Environment protection gates the *whole job*, so approval comes
first and nothing runs before it. `RELEASING.md` steps 4–5 were re-sequenced accordingly and now
carry an **Approval gate** section with the verified settings, who approves and against what
checks, the rationale for `prevent_self_review: false` (single-maintainer pool — the gate is a
deliberate second action, not independent review), and the `gh api` command to re-check the rule.

`tasks/research/conventions.md` carries the same "reviewer-approved environment" wording, but it
is a verbatim research snapshot of `didcomm-dotnet`'s files and is left untouched as a record.
That sibling repo likely has the identical unprotected-environment gap; flagged for its own issue
rather than folded in here.

Residual risk, stated plainly: with one maintainer, `prevent_self_review: false` means the tag
pusher approves their own deployment. The gate stops an *accidental* or *automated* publish and
forces a second deliberate action; it is not independent review. Closing that gap needs a second
trusted reviewer, not a settings change.
